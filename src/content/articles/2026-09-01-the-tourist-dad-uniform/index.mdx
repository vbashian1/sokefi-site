I was in Rome with my wife a few weeks ago. We've been there numerous times so didn't have a particular agenda other than to enjoy some really good food and soak up all that makes Rome, Rome. My wife went for a quick workout while I got ready for the day. Tech pants check, sneakers with heavy-duty tread check. Shirt with sun protection check. Three-way jacket suitable for rain, wind and snow check check and check. I was ready to go.

My wife looked at me and tried not to laugh. She emerged from the bathroom a few minutes later wearing a loose-fitting black dress and flat sandals, a leather jacket and cross-body bag over her shoulders. She was also ready to go. She looked great!

<PullQuote variant="display">Why do we dress like we're ready to trek across a national park when we travel?</PullQuote>

During the day I noticed that many people spoke to her first in Italian — not surprising I told myself — she looks like she lives here. Me on the other hand — definitely not! Everyone spoke to me in English first — clearly I was not one of them. I wasn't even following the "when in Rome" philosophy.

<Inset side="right" pick={0} focus="50% 0%" zoom={1.17} />

What is it with men — especially those from America and the UK? Why do we dress like we're ready to trek across a national park when we travel? We spot each other instantly — sharing knowing glances across the room. We recognise each other. We are part of the same tribe — tech pants or our version of upscale. Ill-fitting jeans, sneakers and a multi-use jacket — we are ready for anything. Even if that means a stroll around a city that all the locals seem to do without being also ready for an expedition into the wilds. I should know. I'm one of them. Maybe even the leader of the pack.

And as leader, I think it's time to try something new. I'm calling it the "What Would George Clooney Wear" checklist. After all, as the once — and still, if you believe my wife — sexiest man in the world, George knows something about how to dress. Imagine the reaction from my wife if instead of the tech pants and three-way jacket, I emerge from the bathroom looking like George Clooney? Okay, well, maybe not quite. But going forward, my checklist will be: would George Clooney wear this? And if the answer is no, I'll choose something else.

And that's my take on the tourist dad uniform.
