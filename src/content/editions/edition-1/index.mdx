Welcome to Sokéfi — a new magazine for midlifers, by midlifers. We created it because the magazine we wanted to read didn't exist. When the idea initially emerged, the very idea of creating an entire magazine — one that focuses on us and where we are now, midlife or perhaps just a bit beyond — seemed like a pipe dream. We've all come from different careers, but what we have in common is a willingness to say yes when everything else is signalling no. We asked each other: isn't this the time we should be winding down our careers rather than jumping into something new? But by then, the idea was baked and away we went.

The name says it all. Kefi is the Greek word for joy, for spirit, for the irrepressible urge to be fully alive — and Sokéfi is our word for chasing it.

<PullQuote variant="display">A new magazine for midlifers, by midlifers.</PullQuote>

We didn't want to read magazines targeting young people — or, for that matter, those that seemed to be for people who weren't living life to its fullest. We didn't want to read articles or blogs on travel by twenty-something travellers — we'd done that — we already knew how to travel as twenty-year-olds. We wanted to be inspired to go to new places, to travel on our own if we wanted to. We wanted to keep up with lifestyle developments that would help us stay healthy going forward. We wanted to be able to say why not rather than no.

To us, Sokéfi is a way of life — not just something to read, but how we want to live it: curious, up for the journey, happy in our own company, intent on living well. It's the life we're after, and everything here grows from it. One promise runs underneath everything: we only write about what we've actually done — the places we've truly been, the questions we're genuinely asking at this stage of life.

Each month we'll bring you new ideas — starting here with [what it means to be 60 today](/articles/what-does-it-mean-to-be-60/). Then we're off: wander with us from a long weekend in [Cairo](/articles/cairo-long-weekend/) to a winter one in [Iceland](/articles/winter-weekend-in-iceland/), down [Highway 61](/articles/blues-highway-highway-61/) to the juke joints where the blues was born, and along the Darwin trail from [Shrewsbury to the Galápagos](/articles/darwin-trail-shrewsbury/). We dive into [what it means to be a solo traveller](/articles/confessions-of-a-modern-nomad/), and why solo travel can be the most satisfying way to travel. And we bring in the man's point of view — in this edition, a humorous take on the [tourist-dad uniform](/articles/the-tourist-dad-uniform/), and a deeper look at the changing relationship between [dads and daughters](/articles/why-my-daughters-are-great-plumbers/).

<PullQuote>We wanted to be able to say why not rather than no.</PullQuote>

In addition to travel, each edition will feature articles on the health side of living well — this time, taking steps to improve our [cognitive health](/articles/optimising-cognitive-abilities/) and new [testing options for colon cancer](/articles/at-home-colon-cancer-screening/). Our goal with these is to raise awareness of the latest developments in the quest for strong, healthy ageing.

And there's so much more besides.

So here's to trying new things. As we like to say, the fun is in the adventure — and we hope you'll come along for the ride.
